# Tomato Care Guide

## Watering
Deep, consistent watering is everything for tomatoes. Irregular watering causes blossom-end rot (black leathery patches) and fruit cracking. Keep moisture 45-85%, watering deeply 2-3 times a week rather than lightly every day — deep watering builds deep roots. Reduce water slightly once fruit starts ripening to concentrate flavour.

## Disease
Early blight and late blight thrive on wet leaves — always water at the base. Humidity above 85% with warm nights is blight weather: prune lower leaves 30cm off the ground, stake plants for airflow. Yellow leaves at the bottom are usually normal aging; yellow with brown spots means blight — remove and destroy those leaves immediately.

## Fertilizer
Heavy feeders. Balanced NPK until flowering, then switch to low-nitrogen, high-potassium (tomato feed). Calcium prevents blossom-end rot — add gypsum at planting. Magnesium deficiency (yellowing between leaf veins) is common: a teaspoon of Epsom salt in water monthly fixes it.

## Temperature
Ideal 18-27°C. Above 35°C pollen dies and flowers drop without setting fruit — shade cloth in peak summer. Night temperatures above 24°C also block fruit set.

## Profit tips
Stake and prune to single or double stem for bigger, earlier fruit that sells at premium. Harvest at "breaker" stage (first blush of colour) — fruit ripens perfectly off the vine and survives transport, cutting losses by a third.
