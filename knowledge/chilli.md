# Chilli Care Guide

## Watering
Chillies tolerate drought better than most vegetables — slight water stress actually increases capsaicin (heat). Keep moisture 35-78%. Overwatering is the common killer: soggy roots rot fast. Let the top soil dry between waterings. During flowering, keep moisture steady or flowers drop.

## Disease
Damping-off kills seedlings in wet soil. Anthracnose (dark sunken fruit spots) spreads in humid rain — pick off infected fruit immediately. Aphids and thrips love chillies: neem oil spray weekly in the evening controls them cheaply.

## Fertilizer
Light feeders early, hungrier at fruiting. Too much nitrogen means huge leafy plants with few chillies. Feed high-potassium when flowers appear. Wood ash worked into soil adds potassium for free.

## Temperature
Ideal 20-32°C — chillies love warmth. Below 15°C growth stops; above 38°C flowers drop. In India they grow nearly year-round except peak winter in the north.

## Profit tips
Regular picking triggers more flowering — harvest green chillies weekly and total yield rises 30-40% versus letting them ripen red. Red ripe chillies sell higher per kg but yield less total weight; split the harvest strategy by market price. Sun-dried surplus chillies store for months and sell in the off-season.
